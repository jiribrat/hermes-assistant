Hermes

Hermes is a private, self-hosted personal assistant used by a single person. It accesses only its own Google account (Calendar and Gmail) on behalf of its owner.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

Hermes je soukromý osobní asistent provozovaný jedním člověkem pro vlastní potřebu. Nejde o veřejnou službu.
