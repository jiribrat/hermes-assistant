Privacy policy

Hermes is a private application used only by its owner. It is not available to the public.

  • Hermes accesses Google user data (Calendar events and Gmail messages) only of its own Google account, solely to perform tasks requested by its owner.
  • Data is processed on the owner's private server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
